## What is Gainable?

Gainable turns the spreadsheets, data and tools your team already uses into working apps. Drop in one sheet or several, and **Gaia**, Gainable's AI, works out how the data fits together, suggests an app and a workflow around it, and builds it. You don't write code or map fields.

Every app comes with its own **Assistant**: an AI teammate that knows the app's data, does work in it, and follows the same access rules as everyone else.

## How it works

1. **Bring your data.** Upload one or more spreadsheets, or connect Google Sheets, Excel Online, HubSpot and other sources. Gaia reads them together as one dataset.
2. **Gaia designs the app.** It suggests pages, views and a workflow, and you can review its decisions before anything is built.
3. **Build and share.** Press Build, invite your team, and change the app any time by describing what you want.
4. **Work with the Assistant.** Everyone in the app can ask its Assistant questions, have it update records, and give it recurring tasks.

You can do all of this in the Gainable builder, or from the AI tool you already work in: Claude, ChatGPT, Claude Code, Codex and others. See [Where you build](/build-from-data#where-you-build).

## Common questions

### Do I need to write code?

No. You describe what you want, or drop in your data, and Gaia builds the app. You change it the same way, by describing the change. See [Changing a built app](/prompting/refining).

### Can I build Gainable apps from Claude or ChatGPT?

Yes. The Gaia MCP connector, for building and code, lets Claude, ChatGPT, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and other MCP clients import data, build apps, change them and publish them, the same way the Gainable builder does. See [the Gaia MCP connector](/mcp/overview).

### Can I work with an app's data from code or another tool?

Yes, through the app's own MCP. Share the app's Assistant and add it to ChatGPT, Claude or any other MCP client to ask about the data, add and update records, and hand it recurring work, with the app's access rules applied. The app's REST API serves its own pages and isn't for outside callers. See [API and programmatic access](/reference/api).

### Can an app stay in sync with Google Sheets?

Yes. An app syncs from the files and sheets it was built from, with approval, Undo and a schedule. Two-way sync writes edits made in the app back to the Google Sheet or Excel Online workbook. See [Keep the app's data current](/manage/data).

### Can I control who sees which records?

Yes. Describe access rules in plain words, such as "Owners only see their own deals", and they apply everywhere in the app, the Assistant included. See [Access, roles and rules](/manage/access).
