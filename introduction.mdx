## What is Gainable?

Gainable turns the spreadsheets, data and tools your team already uses into working apps. Drop in one sheet or several, and **Gaia**, Gainable's AI, works out how the data fits together, suggests an app and a workflow around it, and builds it. You don't write code or map fields.

Every app comes with its own **Assistant**: an AI teammate that knows the app's data, does work in it, and follows the same access rules as everyone else.

## How it works

1. **Bring your data.** Upload one or more spreadsheets, or connect Google Sheets, Excel Online, HubSpot and other sources. Gaia reads them together as one dataset.
2. **Gaia designs the app.** It suggests pages, views and a workflow, and you can review its decisions before anything is built.
3. **Build and share.** Press Build, invite your team, and change the app any time by describing what you want.
4. **Work with the Assistant.** Everyone in the app can ask its Assistant questions, have it update records, and give it recurring tasks.

## Build from Claude and ChatGPT

You don't have to build in the browser. Gaia works inside Claude, ChatGPT, Claude Code, Codex and other AI tools, so you can build, change and publish apps from the chat you already work in. See [Build from Claude and ChatGPT](/build-in-ai-tools).
