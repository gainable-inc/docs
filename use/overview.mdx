You don't have to build apps to use Gainable. This page is for everyone who works in the apps their team built.

## Your Apps grid

Your starting point is the **Apps** grid at [build.gainable.dev/apps](https://build.gainable.dev/apps). It holds every app you have access to, arranged in tabs and groups, with widgets such as **Feed** for team updates and **Files**. Admins can lay out a grid for the whole company.

One sign-in covers every app on the grid.

## Notifications and messages

The bell in the header collects your notifications in one place. The chat icon opens the **Messenger**, where you can message colleagues directly or in groups.

## Working with the Assistant

Every app has its own Assistant. In an app, switch the sidebar from **Browse** to **Assistant** and start a chat, or pick one of the suggested starters. The Assistant reads the app's live data, can add and update records for you, and only sees what you're allowed to see.

Your chats are listed in the sidebar, so you can come back to them. Once a chat is going, you can invite colleagues into it.

## On your phone

Gainable works on your phone. Add it to your home screen on iPhone or Android to get push notifications.

## From your own AI tool

If an app's owner has shared its Assistant, you can connect to it from ChatGPT, Claude or another MCP client and sign in with your Gainable account. Your conversations also show up privately in the app.
