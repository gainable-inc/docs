Every Gainable app is built around your team's data and work, so no two look the same. They all work the same way, though.

## Find your way around

The sidebar has two modes:

- **Browse** lists the app's pages, such as an overview dashboard, boards and tables.
- **Assistant** opens the app's Assistant and your chats with it. See [Work with the Assistant](/use/assistant).

The header has the Messenger, your notifications, and, depending on your access, the app's **Data** and **Settings** pages.

## Records

Select a card on a board, or a row in a table, to open the record. A record has tabs:

- **Info**: the record's fields. Change what you need and select **Save**.
- **Comments**: discuss the record with your team. Type **@** to mention a colleague, who gets a notification, or the app's Assistant, which answers in the thread.
- **Files**: attach files to the record.

To add a record, use the **Add** button at the top of the page, such as **Add Maintenance Request**. Use **Filter** to narrow what a page shows.

## Live for everyone

When a colleague changes something, boards, tables, KPIs and charts update for you straight away, without a reload, and keep your current filter and sort. A cell that someone else changed flashes briefly so you notice it.

If your connection drops, the app catches up by itself when it's back.

## People in records

Owner and assignee fields hold real people in the app, so you see them by name, and "my tasks" means yours. An empty person field reads **Unassigned**.

## What you can see

You see what the app's owners have given you access to. Some records, pages or columns may be hidden from you, and the Assistant follows the same rules when it answers you.
