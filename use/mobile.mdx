---
title: "Gainable on your phone"
description: "Add Gainable to your home screen and get push notifications on iPhone and Android"
---

Gainable works on your phone: the Apps grid, your apps, the Assistant, notifications and messages. Add it to your home screen and it opens like an app, with push notifications for everything that needs you.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Gainable in Safari">
        Go to [build.gainable.dev/apps](https://build.gainable.dev/apps) and sign in.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add it to your Home Screen">
        Tap the Share button, choose **Add to Home Screen**, and tap **Add**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Open it from your Home Screen">
        Open Gainable from its new icon. On iPhone, notifications only work when Gainable is opened this way.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Turn on notifications">
        Tap the bell, then **Turn on**, and allow notifications when your phone asks.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Gainable in Chrome">
        Go to [build.gainable.dev/apps](https://build.gainable.dev/apps) and sign in.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add it to your home screen">
        Open Chrome's menu and choose **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Turn on notifications">
        Open Gainable from your home screen, tap the bell, then **Turn on**, and allow notifications when your phone asks.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## What you get

- **Push notifications** for mentions, messages, Assistant replies and scheduled tasks, even when Gainable is closed. You don't get a push for something you're already looking at.
- **Tap to open.** A notification opens the right app, on the right record or chat.
- **Up to date.** Gainable refreshes itself when you come back to it. Pull down to refresh by hand.

## Turn notifications off

Open the bell and tap **Turn off**. Notifications are set per device, so turning them off on your phone doesn't affect your computer.

If the bell says notifications are blocked, allow them for Gainable in your phone's settings.
