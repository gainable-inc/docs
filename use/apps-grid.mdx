The **Apps** grid is your home in Gainable. It holds the apps you use, arranged the way you like, next to widgets for team updates and files. One sign-in covers every app on it.

Open it at [build.gainable.dev/apps](https://build.gainable.dev/apps), or select **Apps** in your account menu.

## Tabs

The grid is split into tabs along the top. Each tab is its own layout of apps and widgets.

- **Your own tabs** are yours alone. Select **+**, then **New tab**.
- **Account views** are shown to everyone in your Gainable account and are marked with a dot. People with the **Create account views** permission use them to give the whole company the same starting point: an Operations tab, a Sales tab, a tab per team. Select **+**, then **New account view**.
- **Shared views** are tabs your team has shared. Select **+**, then **Browse shared views** to follow one.

A tab's options menu (**⋮**) lets you **Rename** it or **Duplicate to my tabs**, so you can start your own tab from one someone else set up.

## Add apps and widgets

Select **Add**. The **Apps** tab lists the published apps you can open. Select one to put it on the grid. You can add several in a row.

The **Widgets** tab has:

- **Feed**: team updates. Post an update, attach files, and comment on other people's posts.
- **Files**: a shared place for files. Select **Add files** to upload.

Drag tiles and widgets to arrange them, and drag a widget's corner to resize it.

## Groups

Drop one app tile onto another to put them in a group, like a folder on a phone. Select a group to open it and see its apps. Use groups to keep related apps together, such as all of your sales tools.

## Edit and remove

Select the pencil to turn on edit mode. Each tile then shows a remove badge, on the grid and inside open groups. Select the pencil again when you're done.

## Unread badges

A number on an app's tile means something in that app is waiting for you. See [Notifications and messages](/use/notifications).
