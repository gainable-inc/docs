title: "Activity log"

---

Every app keeps an activity log: record changes, builds, rollbacks and undos, data syncs and sign-in changes, each credited to the person who made it. It's kept for 12 months.

## Open the activity log

In the builder, open your app and select the clock icon at the bottom of the left rail. Select an event to see exactly what changed in the **Details** panel, for example a role going from none to Field Staff.

## Filter it

| Filter | Options |
|--------|---------|
| Where | **All**, **In the app** (what people did in the app) or **Builders** (changes made in the builder) |
| What | **All activity**, **Created**, **Updated**, **Deleted**, **Opened the app**, **Builds**, **Code changes** or **Other requests** |
| Who | **Everyone**, or one person |
| Which data | **All collections**, or one table |
| When | **Any time**, **Last 24 hours**, **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days** or **Last 90 days** |

Turn on **Errors only** to see only what went wrong. **Export CSV** downloads what you're looking at.
