---
title: "Share the Assistant"
description: "Let people reach your app's Assistant from ChatGPT, Claude and other AI tools"
---

Your app's Assistant can be used from outside Gainable. When you share it, people add it to ChatGPT, Claude or any other AI tool that supports MCP, sign in with their Gainable account, and ask it questions or have it do work, without opening the app.

Sharing is off until you turn it on.

## Turn on sharing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Connectors dialog">
    In the app, switch the sidebar to **Assistant** and select **Connectors** at the bottom of the chat list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Share tab">
    Turn on **Share this Assistant**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send people the link">
    Select **Copy link to Assistant** and send it to the people who should use it. They add it to their AI tool as a connector. People in the app can also copy the link themselves, from **Connectors**. See [Your apps in Claude and ChatGPT](/use/ai-tools).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What people can do

People who connect get the Assistant's real answers, from the app's live data. The Assistant follows the same access rules as it does in the app: each person sees only what they're allowed to see in the app. Their conversations also appear, privately, in the app.

The link only works for this one Assistant. It doesn't give anyone access to other apps or to the rest of your Gainable account.

## See and remove who is connected

The **Share** tab lists **Connected users**. Turning sharing off disconnects everyone. If you turn it back on, people have to connect again.
