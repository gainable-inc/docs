You don't have to open the builder to build. Gaia works inside the AI tool you already use, so you can turn a spreadsheet into an app, change it and publish it from the same chat where you do the rest of your work.

It's the same Gaia as in the builder: it asks you the same questions, builds the same app, and every change shows up in the builder too.

## What it looks like

Attach a spreadsheet and name Gaia in your first message:

```text
Have Gaia build me an app from this planning sheet
```

Gaia reads the sheet, asks you a few questions about your data and the app, then builds it. After that you just keep talking, without naming Gaia again:

```text
Add a probability column to the deals table
Publish it
```

<Tip>
Say **Gaia** in the first message. Without it, your AI tool may answer the request itself instead of handing it to Gaia. See [Say "Gaia" in your first message](/mcp/overview#say-gaia-in-your-first-message).
</Tip>

## Why build where you already work

- **Your context is already there.** The notes, emails and files in your chat can shape the app.
- **One place for the whole job.** Collect the data, build the app and share it without switching tools.
- **Anyone can pick it up.** An app built in Claude opens in the builder like any other, so someone else can carry on from there.

To work with an app's data from your AI tool once it's built, connect to the app's Assistant instead. See [Your apps in Claude and ChatGPT](/use/ai-tools).
